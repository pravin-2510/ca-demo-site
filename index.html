A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Have another question? We're happy to talk it through.

When should I speak with a Chartered Accountant?

Earlier is usually better: before setting up a business, making a significant financial decision or approaching a filing deadline. A short conversation can help you understand your options.

Can you work with a business outside Delhi?

Yes. We support clients across India through secure digital processes, scheduled calls and clear documentation.

What should I bring to an initial consultation?

Bring a brief description of what you need help with and any relevant notices, filings or financial records you already have. We'll let you know if anything else would be useful.

Do you support startups and newly registered businesses?

Absolutely. We can help you think through business registration, tax and GST obligations, bookkeeping and the compliance calendar that comes with growth.

How do your fees work?

Fees depend on the scope and complexity of the work. After understanding your needs, we'll outline the deliverables, timing and fee before you decide how to proceed.
